Now, why don’t these clinics use the existing AI calling assistants you see in the market? 

Because the current solutions are fundamentally broken for local businesses. To set up a traditional AI bot, a clinic has to buy a virtual VoIP number, register a new corporate SIM, pay massive cloud platform fees, and get billed ₹2 to ₹3 for every single minute the AI speaks. 

For a single clinic, the cure is more expensive than the disease. 

We are SAATHI, and we have built the world’s first local, carrier-free AI receptionist that completely eliminates these costs, keeping the clinic’s existing phone number, and running at virtually zero cost per call."

PART 2: THE BREAKTHROUGH — "CARRIER-FREE AI" (1:30 - 3:30)
================================================================================
[Visual on Slide: Side-by-side comparison of "Traditional AI Bots" vs "SAATHI." Bold text: "Bypassing the Carrier. Zero Cost per Minute."]

[Speaker 2 - Core Innovation]
"So, how did we solve this? We asked ourselves a simple question: why route calls through expensive, complex cloud telephony networks when the clinic already has a phone, a SIM, and a laptop sitting right at the front desk?

Our core innovation is a hardware-free, carrier-free bridge. 

Using Microsoft Phone Link—a tool already pre-installed in every Windows computer—we pair the clinic’s existing smartphone to their desk laptop via Bluetooth. 

When a patient calls the clinic's regular mobile number—the exact same number they have used for 10 years—the call is mirrored to the laptop over Bluetooth. 

SAATHI’s lightweight desktop application intercepts that incoming stream. 

Through highly optimized screen-automation and direct virtual audio loopback, SAATHI auto-accepts the call hands-free and immediately routes the call audio into our processing pipeline.

The patient never knows they aren't speaking to a cloud system. They hear a warm, natural Indian-accented voice that speaks their language—Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, or English. 

But behind the scenes, there is NO Twilio. There is NO Exotel. There is NO per-minute billing. The entire call runs entirely on the clinic’s existing mobile carrier plan. 

We have effectively reduced the marginal cost of AI voice operations by 95%.

If they get 2,000 calls a month, traditional cloud bots cost the clinic ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 in usage bills. With SAATHI, the cost is ₹0."

PART 3: UNDER THE HOOD & LIVE FLOW (3:30 - 5:30)
================================================================================
[Visual on Slide: High-tech architecture diagram. Glowing paths showing: "Call In" -> "Bluetooth" -> "Sarvam STT" -> "Groq LLaMA 3.3 (Intent)" -> "Sarvam TTS" -> "Audio Out" -> "WhatsApp API trigger."]

[Speaker 3 - Technical Pipeline & Flow]
"Let’s look at the engineering under the hood that makes this possible in sub-seconds. To make a voice conversation feel natural, your latency has to be under 1 second. If the patient has to wait 2 seconds after speaking, the illusion is broken.

SAATHI achieves an average conversational latency of under 850 milliseconds. Here is how:

First, we use Silero VAD (Voice Activity Detection) running locally to instantly strip background noise and isolate the patient's speech. 

Second, the audio is processed through Sarvam AI’s localized Speech-to-Text engine, converting Indic dialects into text in real time.

Third, this text is pushed to Groq's high-speed inference engine running LLaMA 3.3-70B. We chose LLaMA 3.3 because it acts as a brilliant, context-aware triage engine. It understands clinic availability, FAQs, and can differentiate an extraction patient in acute pain from a routine checkup.

Finally, the generated response is spoken back to the call using Sarvam’s natural-accented Text-to-Speech synthesizer.

And we don't stop when the call hangs up. 

The moment a patient confirms a booking, SAATHI’s background processor immediately hooks into our WhatsApp dispatch engine. Within 3 seconds of hanging up, the patient receives a beautifully formatted booking confirmation, clinic location coordinates, and calendar invites directly on their WhatsApp. 

For the doctor, SAATHI processes the call transcript, summarizes the consultation, and prepares draft prescription notes for approval, saving them 1.5 hours of tedious manual paperwork every day."

[Visual on Slide: Bold financial metrics. Left: "Traditional Tech Cost: ₹25,000/mo." Right: "SAATHI Cost: ₹1,200/mo." Middle: "SaaS Profit Margin: 80%+"]

[Speaker 4 - Business Model & Numbers]
"Now let's talk about the business, because this is where the opportunity becomes massive for us as a company and for you as an investor.

Because we do not pay for cloud calling infrastructure, we have unlocked a massive cost arbitrage. 

A traditional AI voice agency has to charge a premium just to cover their API costs and VoIP carrier markups. Their floor price is at least ₹15,000 a month, making it impossible to sell to smaller local clinics.

We have zero telephony cost. Our only external costs are tiny fractions of a paisa for LLM tokens on Groq and TTS synthesis. 

This means we can package SAATHI as a highly affordable subscription model for local practices—charging just ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 per month. 

At this price point, it is an absolute no-brainer for a clinic manager. Saving just one missed root-canal booking pays for our entire yearly subscription. 

And for us, the gross margins are astronomical. Out of a ₹3,000 subscription, our actual computational cost is less than ₹500. That is an 83% gross profit margin.

The local healthcare market in India is massive. There are over 3,00,000 private dental clinics, diagnostic centers, and family clinics across tier-1 and tier-2 cities. They are all struggling with front-desk staff turnover, dropped calls, and rising operational costs. 

None of them are served by high-end enterprise VoIP providers. We are the first to bring plug-and-play, affordable AI automation straight to their existing Windows desktops."
