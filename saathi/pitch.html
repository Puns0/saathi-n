01 — Who We Are

We're students who

We are a team of engineering students from SJB Institute of Technology, Kengeri, Bengaluru. At a 24-hour hackathon, we designed and built SAATHI — a complete AI voice assistant — entirely from scratch. No templates. No pre-built bots. Real code, real integrations, real results.

SAATHI started as a solution for rural farmers: get AI advisory over a plain phone call, no internet required on their end. The system handles everything — auto-answering calls, understanding speech in 10+ Indian languages, generating responses, and speaking back — all on a laptop.

A busy clinic shouldn't lose patients
to a busy phone line.

High-volume dental practices face a predictable set of operational problems. Every missed call, every no-show, every manual task is costing time and revenue — and none of it requires a human to solve.

📞

Call Volume Overload

A 10-chair clinic gets dozens of calls daily. One receptionist can't handle simultaneous bookings, FAQs, and reminders without dropping calls.

Missed appointments ↑

No Automated Reminders

Without a reminder system, no-shows pile up — empty chairs, disrupted schedules. Manual calling is time-consuming and inconsistent.

Revenue leakage ↑

Manual Prescription Writing

Doctor's time wasted

03 — Our Core Innovation

We eliminated
the carrier.
Zero cost per call.

Every other AI calling solution needs a Twilio or Exotel VoIP number — that's ₹1–3 per minute, SIM registration, and cloud infrastructure you have to maintain indefinitely.

We found a better way. Using Microsoft Phone Link — already built into Windows — your clinic's existing mobile phone pairs with a laptop via Bluetooth. When a patient calls your regular number, SAATHI intercepts the call on the laptop and handles the entire conversation. The patient never knows anything changed. They just hear a warm, intelligent AI voice.

No new phone number. No new SIM. No per-minute charges. Just plug in and it works.

07 — Product Development Roadmap

Continuous Evolution & robust Stack

Here is how we plan to deploy SAATHI in your clinic over three rapid phases, backed by modern, open-source technology.

Phase 1

Inbound Automation

Week 1–2
  • Deploy Bluetooth gateway to regular clinic phone line.
  • Configure custom FAQ replies & dentist schedule database.

Phase 2

WhatsApp & Integrations
